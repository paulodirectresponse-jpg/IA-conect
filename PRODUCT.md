# IA-conect — Product Context

IA-conect is a commercial AI-content generation platform with authenticated studio workflows for image and video creation, model selection, media references, wallet/credits, history, library, community, settings and admin controls.

## Product priorities

1. Make generation workflows feel fast, clear and trustworthy.
2. Keep model choice understandable without overwhelming the user.
3. Preserve strong visibility of cost, balance and generation state.
4. Support both dark and light themes with equal quality.
5. Treat mobile as a first-class experience.
6. Keep the interface premium without reducing information density or usability.

## Primary surface modes

- Public landing/auth: Persuade.
- Authenticated studio and management screens: Operate.
- Community/gallery: Experience + Operate depending on task.

## Non-negotiables

- Product behavior and financial truth outrank decorative design.
- Auto model selection must not accidentally reveal internal routing when the UI is meant to stay generic.
- Generation controls, references, prices, errors, loading and disabled states must remain explicit.
- Accessibility, responsiveness and performance are part of visual quality.
